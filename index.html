Questions you are probably about to ask

Why not just install one of the self-serve tools?

You can, and plenty of brands do fine that way. The work is not the install. It is writing answers in your voice, testing them against how customers actually phrase things, wiring order lookup, setting escalation rules so it does not confidently answer something it should not, and then tuning it every month as products and policies change. That is about thirty hours up front and a few hours a month forever. If you have those hours, do it yourself.

How is this different from Fin or Zendesk AI?

Those are platforms you license, configure and maintain. Fin bills around $0.99 per resolution on top of seats, so 400 resolutions a month is roughly $400 before anyone has built or maintained anything. We are a flat fee that includes the platform, the build and the ongoing work, and we are not tied to one vendor, so if something better comes along we move you to it.

What does it actually resolve?

Order status, shipping timelines, returns and exchanges, sizing and fit, ingredients and materials, discount codes, stock availability, care instructions. Typically forty to sixty percent of tier one volume, depending on how good your existing content is.

What happens when it does not know?

It says so and hands off to a human with the full conversation attached. It does not guess. Every unanswered question is logged and reviewed in the monthly tuning pass, which is where a lot of the value actually sits.

Will it sound like a robot?

We match it to your existing copy and you approve the tone before launch. If it reads wrong, that is a revision, not a limitation.

What data does it store, and where?

Conversation transcripts and any contact details a customer volunteers during a chat. Stored on US infrastructure. It never touches payment information. We will send you a data processing addendum before you sign rather than after you ask for one.

What if it tells a customer something wrong?

It answers only from content you approved, and anything outside that scope is escalated rather than answered. We review misanswers monthly and we tell you when one happens instead of waiting for you to find it.

How do you measure whether it worked?

We take a baseline before launch: ticket volume, first response time, and conversion rate on the pages the agent will sit on. After launch you get deflection rate, conversations handled, pre-purchase questions captured, contacts passed to Klaviyo, and top unanswered questions. If the numbers do not move, that is a real conversation and not a renewal.

Why should we trust a new agency?

You should not, on faith. That is why two full builds are free to design partners in exchange for the numbers, and why every claim on this page will either end up with a case study behind it or come down.
